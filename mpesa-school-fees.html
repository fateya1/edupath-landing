How It Works

M-Pesa fee payment in 4 steps.

1

Parent opens the Parent Portal

Parents log in to their dedicated portal and view their child's outstanding fee balance for the current term.

2

Clicks "Pay via M-Pesa"

A single tap triggers an M-Pesa STK push to the parent's registered phone number via Safaricom's official Daraja API.

3

Parent enters M-Pesa PIN

The parent confirms the payment on their phone using their M-Pesa PIN. No app download or manual paybill needed.

4

Fee record updates instantly

The balance updates in real time, a digital receipt is generated, and the admin receives an instant notification. A WhatsApp receipt is sent to the parent automatically.

FAQ

M-Pesa fee payment questions

Does Edupath use the official Safaricom Daraja API?

Yes. Edupath SMS is integrated directly with Safaricom's official Daraja API for M-Pesa STK push payments. All transactions are secure, real-time, and compliant with Safaricom's standards.

Do parents need to download an app to pay?

No. Parents pay via M-Pesa STK push triggered from the Parent Portal web app. The STK push appears directly on their phone — no app download required.

What happens if a payment fails?

If an STK push times out or is cancelled, the fee record is not updated and the parent can retry. Failed transactions are logged in the admin dashboard for follow-up.

Can I track which students have outstanding balances?

Yes. The fee management dashboard shows real-time outstanding balances per student, per class, and school-wide. You can filter by term, class, or fee type.
