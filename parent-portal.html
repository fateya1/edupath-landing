FAQ

Parent portal questions

Do parents need to download an app?

No. The parent portal is a fully responsive web app that works on any smartphone browser. An Android app is also available for parents who prefer a native experience with push notifications.

How do parents get their login credentials?

When you add a parent's contact to a student profile, Edupath automatically sends their login credentials via WhatsApp or SMS. No manual onboarding required.

Can one parent account manage multiple children?

Yes. A parent with multiple children at the same school can manage all of them from a single login. Each child's attendance, results, and fees are displayed separately.

Is parent communication monitored by the school?

Yes. All parent-teacher messages sent through the portal are logged and visible to school administrators. This ensures accountability and appropriate communication standards.
