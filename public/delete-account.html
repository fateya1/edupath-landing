Delete your Edupath SMS account

Edupath SMS (mobile and web app)
Edupath Software Engineers and Consultants Ltd, Nairobi, Kenya

You can ask us to delete your Edupath SMS account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This page explains how, what we delete, and what we may have to keep.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to admin@edupath.co.ke with the subject “Delete my Edupath account”. Please write from the email address registered on your account.
  2. In the email, include your full name, your role (parent/guardian, teacher, school administrator), your school's name, and the email address or phone number registered on your account. Never include your password.
  3. We may reply to confirm your identity before acting on the request, to make sure no one else can delete your account.
  4. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email once it is done.

You can also ask us to delete specific data without closing your account, using the same email address.

What we delete

  • Your profile details (name, email address, phone number)
  • Your login credentials and any biometric-login settings tied to your account
  • Notification and device information linked to your account
  • Usage and technical data linked to your account, where we can identify it

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Payroll and payment/transaction records, which we must keep under Kenyan tax, employment and financial record-keeping law (currently at least 7 years)
  • Regulator Portal access audit logs, kept for accountability for a minimum of 24 months
  • Records a School holds as the data controller, such as student academic and attendance records. These are managed by the School, so please contact the School to request correction or deletion. The School may then instruct us to carry it out.

Anything we keep is used only for the legal or accountability purpose above, and is deleted or anonymised once the retention period ends.

Closing a School account

School administrators who want to close their School's Edupath account can use the same email address. We keep the data for a short transition period so it can be exported, then delete or anonymise it unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
