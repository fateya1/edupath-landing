FAQ

Attendance tracking questions

Can teachers mark attendance from their phones?

Yes. The teacher portal is fully responsive and works on any smartphone browser. Teachers can also use the Edupath Android app with offline support for areas with poor connectivity.

How quickly do parents receive absence alerts?

Parents receive a WhatsApp alert within seconds of a teacher marking a student absent. The message includes the student's name, date, and the school's name.

Can I generate attendance reports for NEMIS?

Yes. Edupath generates attendance reports per student and per class for any date range. These can be exported and used for NEMIS reporting and compliance purposes.

What happens if there's no internet when marking attendance?

The Edupath Android app supports offline attendance marking. Teachers mark the register without internet, and all records sync automatically when the device reconnects.
