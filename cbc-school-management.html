FAQ

Common questions about CBC management

Does Edupath support both CBC and 8-4-4?

Yes. Edupath SMS fully supports both Kenya's CBC curriculum and the legacy 8-4-4 system. You can run both in the same school, and report cards are generated correctly for either system.

Which CBC pathways are supported?

All four senior school pathways are natively supported: STEM, Humanities, Arts & Sports Science, and Social Sciences. Students are assigned a pathway during admission and all assessments are mapped accordingly.

How are CBC grades calculated?

Edupath automatically maps scores to the four CBC competency levels: EE (Exceeds Expectation), ME (Meets Expectation), AE (Approaches Expectation), and BE (Below Expectation) based on configurable score thresholds.

Can I generate report cards for the whole school at once?

Yes. Edupath generates PDF report cards for all students in your school in seconds — whether you have 50 or 800 students. Report cards include CBC grades, rankings, attendance summary, and teacher comments.
