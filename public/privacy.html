Data Protection & Privacy Policy

Edupath School Management System (Edupath SMS)
A product of Edupath Software Engineers and Consultants Ltd, Nairobi, Kenya

Version 1.0 · Effective date: 13 October 2026

1. Introduction and Purpose

Edupath Software Engineers and Consultants Ltd (“Edupath,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Edupath School Management System (“Edupath SMS” or the “Platform”), a cloud-based, multi-tenant platform used by schools, teachers, parents, students, and government education regulators in Kenya. This policy explains what personal data the Platform processes, why, on what legal basis, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the rights available to the people it describes.

This policy is written to align with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (“the Act”) and the data protection regulations issued under it, and to reflect how Edupath SMS is actually built — including its multi-tenant architecture, its M-Pesa payment integration, and its Government Regulator Portal. It applies to the Edupath SMS web application, the Edupath SMS mobile application, and the www.edupath.co.ke marketing site.

3. Our Role: Controller and Processor

Edupath's relationship to personal data on the Platform is not uniform, and this policy treats the two roles separately because the Act requires different obligations of each:

  • As data processor: for student records, academic results, attendance, fee balances, and staff records that a School enters into Edupath SMS, the School is the data controller and Edupath acts as a processor, handling that data strictly on the School's instructions and under the terms of the School Onboarding Agreement between Edupath and the School.
  • As data controller: for account and billing information Edupath collects directly — School administrator contact details, subscription and payment records, support communications, and platform usage analytics — Edupath is the data controller and determines the purpose and means of that processing itself.

Where this policy refers to “we process” or “we use,” the specific role (controller or processor) follows the categorisation above unless stated otherwise.

6. How We Collect Data

  • Directly from Schools: administrators enter or bulk-import student, staff, and academic data during onboarding and ongoing use.
  • Directly from parents/guardians and staff: through account registration, the parent portal, and consent forms.
  • Automatically: log-in timestamps, device information, and usage analytics collected as users interact with the web and mobile applications.
  • From payment processors: M-Pesa transaction confirmations received via the Safaricom Daraja API when a fee, canteen, or other payment is made.

7. How We Use Data

  • To provide the core Platform: enrolment, gradebook, timetabling, attendance, fee management, HR/payroll, and communication features.
  • To process payments and reconcile fee, canteen, and procurement accounts.
  • To send notifications and communications a School or parent has requested (WhatsApp, SMS, email, in-app).
  • To power the Government Regulator Portal for Schools that have explicitly opted in (Section 9).
  • To maintain security, investigate misuse, and comply with legal obligations.
  • To improve the Platform, using aggregated or de-identified usage data wherever feasible.

Edupath does not use student, parent, or staff personal data to train third-party artificial intelligence models, and does not sell personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

9. The Government Regulator Portal: How Data Sharing Works

The Regulator Portal gives authorised MOE Sub-County and County Directors of Education, and TSC officials, visibility into participating Schools' enrolment, staffing, and compliance data. Because this is the most sensitive data-sharing relationship on the Platform, it operates under additional safeguards:

  • Opt-in only: a School's data is never visible on the Regulator Portal unless an authorised School administrator has explicitly enabled data sharing for that School.
  • Aggregated and summary data: the Portal is designed to show enrolment counts, teacher numbers, and a compliance scorecard rather than individual student academic records or disciplinary history.
  • Access controls: regulator users authenticate with two-factor (OTP) login and are subject to a 30-minute session timeout.
  • Audit trail: all regulator access to School data is logged, and Schools may request a record of which regulator users have accessed their data.
  • Withdrawal: a School may withdraw its consent to Regulator Portal data sharing at any time; access ceases from the point of withdrawal, though historical audit logs of prior access are retained for accountability.

10. International and Cross-Border Data Transfers

Edupath's infrastructure providers (Vercel and Neon) may process and store data on servers located outside Kenya. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, Edupath relies on the transfer mechanisms permitted under section 48 of the Act — including contractual safeguards with its infrastructure providers and, where applicable, transfers to jurisdictions recognised as having adequate data protection frameworks. Edupath is working toward selecting infrastructure regions and contractual terms that minimise cross-border transfer of Kenyan student data, and will update this policy as that work progresses.

11. Data Security Measures

  • Multi-tenant data isolation: every School's data is scoped server-side by a school identifier derived from authenticated user credentials, never from client-supplied input, preventing one School from accessing another's data.
  • Encryption in transit for all Platform traffic (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Role-based access control, so that a user (parent, teacher, administrator, or regulator) can only see the data their role permits.
  • Two-factor authentication for Regulator Portal access, and standard authentication (with biometric option on mobile) for School accounts.
  • Regular review of third-party processor security practices.
  • Internal access to production data is limited to personnel who require it to operate and support the Platform.

13. Children's Data and Special Protections for Minors

The majority of data subjects on Edupath SMS are students who are minors under Kenyan law. Edupath treats this as the single most sensitive category of data on the Platform and applies the following additional protections, consistent with the Act and the Children Act:

  • Student accounts and data are created and managed by the School and, where applicable, by a parent/guardian — minors do not independently create Edupath accounts.
  • Parental/guardian consent is sought, through the School, before a student's contact or communication data (for example, a parent's WhatsApp number) is used for notifications.
  • Student data is never used for marketing, advertising, or any purpose beyond the educational and administrative functions the School has engaged Edupath to provide.
  • Access to student records is restricted by role: a parent can generally see only their own child's records, and a teacher only the classes they are assigned to.

14. Data Subject Rights

Under the Act, data subjects (or, for minors, their parent/guardian) have the right to:

  • Be informed of how their personal data is processed (this policy, and the School's own communications, are intended to satisfy this).
  • Access the personal data held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated data.
  • Request deletion of their data, subject to legal retention obligations (Section 12).
  • Object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for any processing based on consent (for example, Regulator Portal data sharing or WhatsApp notifications), without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya.

Because most student and staff data is controlled by the School rather than Edupath directly, requests to exercise these rights should generally be directed to the relevant School in the first instance; the School may in turn instruct Edupath, as its processor, to action the request. Requests relating to account or billing data that Edupath controls directly may be sent to the contact in Section 18.

15. Consent and Withdrawal of Consent

Where processing relies on consent — notably, Regulator Portal data sharing and parent/guardian communications — consent is collected through a clear, opt-in mechanism at the point of registration or School onboarding, and can be withdrawn at any time through the Platform or by contacting the School or Edupath directly. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal, and does not affect processing carried out on a different legal basis (for example, statutory payroll processing).

16. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to the rights of data subjects, Edupath will notify the ODPC within the timeframe required by the Act (currently 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, where feasible), and will notify affected Schools without undue delay so that Schools can, in turn, inform affected parents, guardians, and staff as required. Edupath maintains an internal incident response process to investigate, contain, and remediate any suspected breach.

17. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Edupath SMS web application and the www.edupath.co.ke marketing site use cookies and similar technologies for authentication (keeping a user logged in), security, and basic usage analytics. Edupath does not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers. The mobile application does not use browser cookies but may store limited data locally on-device to support offline functionality and biometric login.

19. Complaints to the Regulator

Data subjects who believe their personal data has been processed unlawfully, or who are dissatisfied with Edupath's or a School's response to a rights request, may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), Kenya, through the contact channels published on the ODPC's official website.
