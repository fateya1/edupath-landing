How Kenyan schools reduced fee arrears by 60% using M-Pesa automation

March 28, 2026 · 6 min read · By Edupath SMS Team

Outstanding fee balances are one of the most persistent challenges facing Kenyan school administrators. Bursars spend hours every week calling parents, chasing payments, and reconciling manual records — time that would be better spent supporting students.

Why fee arrears are so persistent

The root causes of fee arrears in most Kenyan schools are predictable:

  • Parents forget. Without regular reminders, fee due dates pass unnoticed.
  • Payment is inconvenient. Travelling to school or finding a paybill number adds friction.
  • No visibility. Parents don't know their exact outstanding balance without calling the school.
  • Manual follow-up doesn't scale. A bursar can't call 300 parents every week.

M-Pesa automation addresses all four of these problems simultaneously.

The workflow that works

1. Parents see their balance in the Parent Portal

Every parent has access to a dedicated portal where they can see their child's current term fee balance, payment history, and outstanding amount — at any time, from any device, without calling the school.

2. Automated daily reminders via WhatsApp

Every morning at 8 AM EAT, parents with outstanding balances receive a WhatsApp message with their child's name, the outstanding amount, and the due date. The message includes a direct prompt to pay. No manual action from the bursar is required — the system sends these automatically.

💬 Sample reminder: "Dear Parent, your child Amina Wanjiku has an outstanding balance of KES 12,500 for Term 1 2026. Due: 31 March 2026. Log in to pay via M-Pesa: app.edupath.co.ke"

3. One-tap M-Pesa STK push payment

When a parent is ready to pay, they open the Parent Portal and click "Pay via M-Pesa." An STK push appears on their phone immediately. They enter their M-Pesa PIN and the payment is complete — no paybill numbers, no manual confirmation, no waiting.

4. Instant receipt and balance update

The moment payment is confirmed, the fee record updates in real time, a digital receipt is sent to the parent via WhatsApp, and the admin dashboard reflects the new balance. The bursar sees everything without lifting a finger.

Why WhatsApp reminders outperform SMS

Kenyan parents are active WhatsApp users. Messages sent via WhatsApp Business API (Twilio) have significantly higher open and response rates than traditional SMS, and they allow richer formatting — including the student's name, exact balance, due date, and a payment link in a single message. Parents find it more personal and are more likely to act on it.

Setting escalation reminders

For persistent outstanding balances, Edupath sends escalating reminders at 30 days, 7 days, and 3 days before the due date — with increasing urgency in the message tone. After the due date, daily reminders continue until the balance is cleared or a payment plan is arranged.
